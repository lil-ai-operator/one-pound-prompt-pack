# AI Sales Image Prompt Pack

A ready-to-sell £1 digital product pack for makers, freelancers, coaches, small shops, and service providers who need better sales images without hiring a designer.

This product is intentionally simple: one useful prompt pack, a clean sales page, listing copy, fulfillment email, and outreach posts. It is original, brand-neutral, does not copy copyrighted templates, and makes no income promises.

## What Is Included

- `product/ai-sales-image-prompt-pack.md` — the customer-facing product
- `sales-page/index.html` — a polished one-page sales page
- `listing-copy.md` — marketplace listing copy for Gumroad, Lemon Squeezy, Ko-fi, Payhip, or similar
- `fulfillment-email.md` — delivery email for buyers
- `outreach-posts.md` — ethical posts and DMs to promote the product

## Suggested Price

£1.

The product is priced for impulse purchase, quick trust, and easy delivery. It should feel like a tiny, practical shortcut rather than a big promise.

## Positioning

Use this phrase:

> A practical prompt pack for creating clearer, more persuasive sales images with AI image tools.

Avoid claims like:

- "Guaranteed sales"
- "Make money fast"
- "Secret prompts"
- "Viral templates"
- "Copy this winning ad"

1. Upload the markdown product as a PDF or downloadable file.

4. Post 3-5 examples from `outreach-posts.md`.
