# AI Sales Image Prompt Pack

Create clearer, sharper sales images with AI image tools.

This pack gives you practical prompt recipes for product graphics, offer images, social posts, and visual hooks. The prompts are inspired by general direct-response principles: clarity, contrast, specificity, proof, relevance, and a single clear idea per image.

No copyrighted templates. No income promises. No magic. Just useful prompts you can adapt quickly.

## How To Use This Pack

1. Pick the prompt that matches your offer.
2. Replace the bracketed text with your details.
3. Paste it into your AI image tool.
4. Generate 3-5 versions.
5. Choose the clearest image, not the fanciest one.
6. Add final text manually in Canva, Figma, Photoshop, or your preferred editor if your AI tool struggles with typography.

## Prompt Tips

- Keep one main idea per image.
- Use plain language.
- Make the product or outcome visually obvious.
- Use contrast so the image works at small sizes.
- Avoid clutter, tiny details, and long text inside the image prompt.
- For ads or social posts, leave clean space where you can add a headline later.

## Prompt Variables

Use these placeholders:

- `[PRODUCT]` — your product or service
- `[AUDIENCE]` — who it is for
- `[PROBLEM]` — the pain point or friction
- `[DESIRED RESULT]` — the practical outcome
- `[STYLE]` — clean, premium, playful, editorial, minimal, bold, etc.
- `[FORMAT]` — square post, story, banner, thumbnail, product mockup, etc.
- `[BRAND COLORS]` — your colors
- `[MOOD]` — calm, confident, urgent, friendly, expert, etc.
- `[OBJECTION]` — a concern your buyer may have before purchasing

## 1. Clear Offer Image

Use when you need a simple image that makes the offer understandable at a glance.

```text
Create a clean sales image for [PRODUCT], made for [AUDIENCE]. Show the product as the clear focus, with visual cues that suggest [DESIRED RESULT]. Use a [STYLE] style, strong contrast, simple composition, and enough empty space for a short headline. Avoid clutter, fake screenshots, exaggerated claims, and unreadable text. Format: [FORMAT]. Brand colors: [BRAND COLORS].
```

## Simple Testing Checklist

Before publishing an image, ask:

- Can someone understand the offer in 3 seconds?
- Is there one clear focal point?
- Does the image avoid fake proof or exaggerated claims?
- Would this still work as a small thumbnail?
- Is any text readable and accurate?
- Does it feel relevant to the intended buyer?

## Final Reminder

AI-generated images are starting points. The best sales image is not the most complex image. It is the one that makes the right buyer understand the offer faster.
